STATUS: Fictional policy for AIP444 context-engineering experiments; not an actual institution policy.

The main computer lab is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays. Students must sign in with their student account. Printing is limited to 30 black-and-white pages per student per week. Food is not permitted near lab computers. Technical problems should be reported to the campus IT help desk.
