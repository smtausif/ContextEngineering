Printing and Copying Services
Fictional College Policy | AIP444 Test Dataset
Document ID: 19

Students receive a fictional $8 printing credit at the start of each term. Black-and-white printing costs $0.10 per page and colour printing costs $0.40 per page. Unused printing credit expires at the end of the term and cannot be transferred. Print jobs left uncollected for more than 24 hours are deleted from the queue.

Note: This policy is fictional and is intended only for a controlled AI research experiment.
