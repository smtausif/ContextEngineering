Accessibility Accommodations
Fictional College Policy | AIP444 Test Dataset

Students requesting exam accommodations should submit supporting documentation at least 10 business days before the exam. Approved extra-time accommodations are applied according to each student’s individual plan. Requests for accessible classroom equipment should be submitted five business days before it is needed. Accommodation details are shared only with authorized staff who need the information.
