DOCUMENT: Attendance and Participation
DOCUMENT ID: 03_attendance_and_participation.txt
STATUS: Fictional policy for AIP444 context-engineering experiments; not an actual institution policy.

Students are expected to attend scheduled classes and participate in group activities. An attendance warning is issued after three unexcused absences in the same course. Participation contributes 5% to the final grade in courses that use the standard participation rubric. Missing a class does not automatically excuse missed assignments. Students should contact their instructor about missed learning activities.
